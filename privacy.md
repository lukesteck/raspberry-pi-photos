# Privacy Policy

Raspberry Pi Photos is a personal-use application that uploads photographs from a Raspberry Pi to the user's Google Photos account.

The application accesses Google Photos only to perform these backups.

Google OAuth credentials are stored locally on the Raspberry Pi. Photographs are uploaded directly to Google Photos using Google's API.

The application does not sell user information or share photographs with unrelated third parties.

Users can revoke Google Photos access through their Google Account settings.
